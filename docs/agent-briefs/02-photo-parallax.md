# A photograph that feels dimensional

Use the picture and depth map in `examples/comp/03-media`. 5 seconds: a slow push-in with depth parallax, film grain and
a vignette, and a caption that fades up at 1.5 s without covering the subject. The picture must not be shown larger than
its pixels (no `upscaled-media` warning).

Reviewer assertions: `moves photo 0-5`, `visible_by caption 2.0`, `no_overlap caption subject-box`.
